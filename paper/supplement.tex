\section{Pillar angles and shoes}\label{sec:rigangles}

The leg offset, in units of the subject's height in pixels, is the mean horizontal coordinate of the right lower-leg
polygon's vertices minus the mean horizontal coordinate of the left lower-leg polygon's vertices. For each camera, we
took the median of the leg offset over subjects. For each pillar, we
averaged the cameras' medians over the pillar's cameras. The leg offset is negative in pillar A, which means that the
subject's right leg appears on the left of the image, so pillar A faces the subject's front. By the
$\cos\theta$ shortening that the main text describes, the ratio of a pillar's leg offset to pillar A's leg offset is
about 0.71 at $\pm45^\circ$, 0 at $\pm90^\circ$, $-0.71$ at $\pm135^\circ$ and $-1$ at $180^\circ$. Figure~\ref{M-fig:rig}(a) in
the main text prints each pillar's measured ratio, which lies nearest the ratio for the angle at which the main text
places the pillar.

To test whether the subjects wore shoes, we paired each own-outfit photograph of a subject with the same camera's
photograph of the subject in the measurement suit. For each pair, we divided the height in pixels in the outfit by the
height in pixels in the suit. For each own outfit, we took the median of this ratio over the pairs.

\section{Self-reported stature and weight}\label{sec:selfreport}

Because a customer reports stature and weight with error, we added self-report errors to stature and weight in
\nDraws\ random draws and repeated the main variant's baseline and width models in each draw. The errors are
normal, with the standard deviations of \selfReportSdCm~cm and \selfReportSdKg~kg that Bartol et al.\
assumed.\cite{bartol2022} In each draw, the stature with its error also sets the scale $S$. With the errors added, the
baseline model's MAPE rises from \mapeBaseline\% to \selfBaseMin--\selfBaseMax\% over the draws. In the same
draws, the widths lower the baseline model's MAPE by \selfRedSuitMin--\selfRedSuitMax~pp in the suit and by
\selfRedOwnMin--\selfRedOwnMax~pp in the own outfits (Table~\ref{tab:selfreport}). With the errors added, the \ciLevel\% confidence
interval of each own outfit's reduction and that of its shortfall lie above zero in every draw.
